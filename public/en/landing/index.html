Your 30 days, week by week

Week 1

2-minute wins

Tiny tasks that let your brain practice starting and finishing: put away three things, clear one surface, send that message you've been putting off.

Week 2

Fewer distractions

Mute notifications, move your social apps out of reach, break your biggest task into 3 steps, and do your first 25-minute block with no interruptions.

Week 3

Consistency without willpower

Get your space ready for tomorrow, do the urgent thing before you check your phone, and learn to talk to yourself kindly when you slip up.

Week 4

Closing what's still open

Finish something you left half-done, spend a morning off social media, and compare day 1 with today. If you want, repeat the plan.
