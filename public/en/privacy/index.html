Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

Luz Stella Bermudez, owner of the StartNow brand, based in Colombia. Contact: ellie@eleanorgrantofficial.com.

2. What data is used and where it stays

  • Quiz answers, name, and email: saved only in your browser to show you your result and set up your plan in the app. They are not sent to our servers, and we don't use them to email you.
  • Progress in the app: also saved only in your browser.
  • Purchase data (name, email, phone, and payment details): collected and processed by Hotmart at checkout, under its own privacy policy. We receive from Hotmart the information needed to deliver the product and support you.
  • Ad measurement: we use the Meta pixel (Facebook and Instagram), which records visits and actions on these pages (for example, reaching the checkout page) to measure and improve our ads. Meta handles that data under its own policy.
  • Approximate country: to show you the price in your currency, we check your approximate country through Cloudflare. We don't store that information.

3. What we use it for

  • Showing you your profile and your 30-day plan.
  • Processing your purchase and delivering the product (through Hotmart).
  • Answering your questions and refund requests.
  • Measuring how our ads perform.

We don't sell your personal data.

4. Your rights

You can ask to access, update, correct, or delete your personal data, and withdraw your consent for us to use it, by emailing ellie@eleanorgrantofficial.com. We handle personal data in line with Colombian data protection law (Law 1581 of 2012).

Because your quiz answers and progress are saved in your browser, you can delete them yourself at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, or with the "Reset everything" option inside the app.

5. Minors

StartNow is intended for people 18 and older.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always the one published on this page, with its last-updated date.
